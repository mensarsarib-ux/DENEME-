Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Hesabım Muhasebe iPhone app, why and how we use it, where we store it, and your rights. By using the app you accept this policy.

1. Data controller

The app is operated by Muhammet Ensar Sarıboğa. Contact: destek@hesabimmuhasebe.com.

2. Data we collect

The following data is processed so the app can work. It is kept linked to your account.

Data typeWhat
Contact infoEmail address; optionally your name, phone number, business name, address and contact details
Financial recordsSales, purchases, collections and payments, amounts, dates, cash and bank balances, customer and supplier receivable/payable balances
User contentCustomer and supplier records, notes
Account and device identifiersAccount ID, identifiers of connected devices and their last access time

The app does not use your contacts, location, camera, microphone, health data or advertising identifier.

3. Why we use data

  • To let you sign in, sync your data between your devices and make backups.
  • To run app features such as customer balances, cash, reports and receivables tracking.
  • To handle security and support matters related to your account.

Your data is not used for advertising, not shared with other apps or companies for tracking, and never sold.

4. Storage and security

Your data is stored on your device and, when you sign in with your account, in your account hosted on Supabase infrastructure. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Only you and the team members you authorize can access your account.

5. Third-party service providers

  • Supabase: account, authentication and data hosting.
  • Apple: subscriptions and purchases (the app never sees your payment details).
  • Email service provider: delivery of verification codes and password reset emails.

These providers only access the data needed to provide their service.

6. How data is collected and legal basis

Data is collected electronically from the information you enter into the app and automatically while you use it (for example, connected device information). We process it because it is necessary to perform our contract with you (providing your account and the app service), to comply with our legal obligations, and for our legitimate interests in security and preventing abuse, provided this does not override your fundamental rights and freedoms (Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, Article 5(2); GDPR Article 6(1)(b), (c) and (f)).

7. International transfers

The servers of the providers above (data hosting, email delivery and Apple's payment system) may be located outside Türkiye, in the European Union and the United States. Your data may therefore be transferred abroad in order to provide the service, subject to the safeguards required by Article 9 of Law No. 6698 and, where applicable, the GDPR.

8. Data about other people that you enter

For the information you record about your customers and suppliers (name, phone, email, address, transactions), you are the data controller; we only store this data on your behalf in order to provide the service (data processor). It is your responsibility to inform these people and to have a legal basis before recording their data.

9. Retention

Your data is kept while your account is open. So that data deleted by mistake by a team member can be recovered, the state of business data before each of its last 30 changes is also kept; these copies are deleted together with the business or the account. If you delete your account, your account information and cloud data are permanently deleted. Data on your device can be cleared at any time with "Delete all data".

10. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and all your cloud data from within the app: Settings → Delete my account (at the bottom of the page; your password is required). Deletion cannot be undone. Businesses you own and the memberships in them are also deleted.

11. Subscriptions and purchases

Paid plans are charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period. You can cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. The app never sees or stores your payment details.

12. Children

The app is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from people under 18.

13. Your rights (KVKK and GDPR)

You have the rights granted by Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 and, if you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, by the GDPR. These include learning whether your data is processed, requesting information, access and a copy, correction, deletion, restriction, objecting to processing and data portability. You can access your data, make backups and delete your account directly in the app. For other requests, use the contact address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

14. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. For significant changes we will notify you in the app, and the date on this page will be updated.

15. Contact

For privacy questions and requests: destek@hesabimmuhasebe.com
