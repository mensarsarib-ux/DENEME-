Frequently asked questions

How do I add a new customer or supplier?

Tap the + button at the top right of the home screen. The record is added to the open tab (Customers, Suppliers or a tab you created).

How do I enter a transaction?

On the person's page, tap SALE, PURCHASE, COLLECTION or PAYMENT at the bottom, type the amount and tap ADD.

Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored in your account on secure servers and synced between the phones you sign in on with the same account. You can also back up and restore a file in Settings → Data.

How do I work with my teammates?

The team feature is available on Pro and higher plans. Your teammate signs in to the app with their own account and sends a join request using your email address; once you approve it, you use your business's data together.

I forgot my password.

Tap "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen. A password reset link is sent to your email address.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. The app is available in 17 languages; the choice applies only to that phone.

Plans and subscriptions

  • The Free plan can be used without time limit. Pro, Pro Plus and Premium are yearly subscriptions.
  • Payment is taken through your Apple Account; the app never sees your card details.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period.
  • Cancel: on your iPhone, Settings → Apple Account (your name) → Subscriptions → Hesabım Muhasebe → Cancel Subscription. Or manage it from this link.
  • Restore: if you changed phones, tap "Upgrade" on the plan card at the top of the menu, then "Restore purchases".
  • Refunds: refund requests are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap "Delete my account" at the bottom of the Settings page. Your account and all your data on the server are permanently deleted. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription; cancel it separately with the steps above.
